Address detective

Paste what ipconfig /all says about your computer. The detective labels your MAC, IPv4 and IPv6 addresses and your default gateway, names your network card’s manufacturer, and shows every address in binary.

1Open Command Prompt (Start, type cmd, press Enter).
2Type ipconfig /all | clip and press Enter. That copies the whole output.
3Click in the box below and paste (Ctrl+V). No Command Prompt? Press Use a sample.
Everything stays on this page. Only the Look it up online button sends anything: the first half of one MAC address.
Converter: one byte in denary, hex and binary

Click the bits to flip them. One hex digit is exactly 4 bits, which is why MAC and IPv6 addresses are written in hex.

Detective questions
  1. What is your MAC address, and which company made your network card?
  2. What is your IPv4 address? Is it private or public? How can you tell?
  3. Which part of your IPv4 address is the network and which is the host? Compare with your partner: which part is the same?
  4. What is your default gateway, and which device is it?
  5. Your IPv6 address starts fe80. What does that tell you?
